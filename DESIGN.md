# Design

User direction, 2026-11-08: Frame must look and read like separate traditional photo books; Photo must feel like albums. This supersedes the initial hero-led photography landing page.

Frame: three cloth-colored covers, inset photographs, small colophon, visible spine and paper edge. Interior: warm white paper, a quiet center gutter, generous margins, paired photographs and folios. Click or keyboard to turn pages; full-screen for detail. Mobile stacks the two pages without cropping the original images.

Photo: year/month index, stacked-print album covers, event dates and counts; inside, closely spaced contact-sheet rows with original proportions. No oversized marketing hero. Curation: paginated 60-photo grid, explicit included/excluded state, visible reason and manual override, batch actions and persistent local saves.

Real photos only. Warm paper background, dark green ink, local Newsreader plus system Chinese serif. Focus states, reduced-motion support, labeled controls, responsive touch targets and semantic HTML. No generated imagery.
